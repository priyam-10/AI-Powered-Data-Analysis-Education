# 📚 Research Sources & Supporting Material

This document lists the authoritative sources used for the research and analysis presented in the **AI-Powered Data Analysis** report.

The project focuses on the impact of Artificial Intelligence on Education, including adoption trends, opportunities, institutional readiness, and responsible AI use.

---

## 1. OECD — AI Use by Individuals Across the OECD

**Organization:** Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)

**Purpose:**  
Used to support statistics on Generative AI adoption among individuals and students across OECD countries.

**Key data used:**
- More than one-third of individuals across OECD countries used Generative AI in 2025.
- Around 75% of students aged 16 and above reported using Generative AI in 2025.

🔗 **Official Source:**  
https://www.oecd.org/en/about/news/announcements/2026/01/ai-use-by-individuals-surges-across-the-oecd-as-adoption-by-firms-continues-to-expand.html

---

## 2. OECD — Digital Education Outlook 2026

**Organization:** Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)

**Purpose:**  
Used to understand the role of Artificial Intelligence and digital technologies in education, including opportunities, challenges, and responsible implementation.

**Key themes used:**
- AI-enabled education
- Personalized learning
- Teacher support
- Digital transformation
- Responsible and effective use of AI in education

🔗 **Official Source:**  
https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-digital-education-outlook-2026_062a7394-en.html

---

## 3. Pew Research Center — Teens, Social Media and AI Chatbots 2025

**Organization:** Pew Research Center

**Purpose:**  
Used to support statistics related to AI chatbot adoption among teenagers in the United States.

**Key data used:**
- 64% of U.S. teens aged 13–17 reported having used AI chatbots.
- 68% of teens aged 15–17 reported using AI chatbots.

🔗 **Official Source:**  
https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/12/09/teens-social-media-and-ai-chatbots-2025/

---

## 4. UNESCO — Guidance for Generative AI in Education and Research

**Organization:** United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO)

**Purpose:**  
Used to support the discussion of responsible and human-centered use of Generative AI in education.

**Key themes used:**
- Responsible AI adoption
- Human oversight
- AI literacy
- Privacy and data protection
- Ethical considerations
- Risks associated with Generative AI

🔗 **Official Source:**  
https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research

---

## 5. UNESCO — Survey on Formal AI Guidance in Schools and Universities

**Organization:** UNESCO

**Purpose:**  
Used to highlight the gap between the growing use of AI and institutional readiness to provide formal guidance.

**Key data used:**
- UNESCO's global survey covered more than 450 schools and universities.
- Fewer than 10% of surveyed institutions had formal guidance on Generative AI in 2023.

🔗 **Official Source:**  
https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-survey-less-10-schools-and-universities-have-formal-guidance-ai
